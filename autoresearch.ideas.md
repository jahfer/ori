- **Push-based wakeup for promises/channels**: Instead of polling `process_blocked_fibers` every iteration, when a promise is resolved or channel gets a value, directly schedule the waiting fiber. Would require resources to know about their scope/scheduler. High impact but architectural change. Careful: `blocked_dirty` flag approach failed due to missed wakeups.
- **Fiber pool**: Reuse fibers across tasks instead of creating new ones. Fiber.new is a C-level allocation; pooling could save significant overhead for fork_join workloads. Complex to implement correctly — fibers can't be reset in Ruby.
- **Optimize Select.await further**: The inner Ori.sync still creates a full scope with Mutex, Fiber.set_scheduler etc. Could use a lighter-weight mechanism for the race.
- **YJIT insight**: Direct ivar access (@pending, @blocked etc) is SLOWER than accessor methods. YJIT optimizes small methods better than inline ivar reads. Keep using methods.
